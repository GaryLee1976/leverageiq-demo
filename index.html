AI-Generated Meeting Brief

Turn account context into a concise preparation document.

NorthStar Distribution • Executive Conversation

Current relationship: Healthy • $2.4M ARR • Executive sponsor engaged
Signal: Adoption increased across two facilities during the last 60 days.
Hypothesis: Operational growth may create a need for another deployment.
Objective: Understand what is driving adoption and whether expansion supports NorthStar's priorities.
Lead with: Customer outcomes and curiosity—not a product pitch.
Suggested opener: “I noticed adoption has accelerated across the network. What’s driving that change, and where is your team feeling the most operational pressure?”
Desired outcome: Mutually agreed discovery session with relevant operational and executive stakeholders.

Why Gary Built This

“I built this concept to demonstrate how I think about strategic account management: use technology to surface the right information, then use human relationships, curiosity and judgment to create customer value.”

Technology should remove friction.

Less time assembling information. More time understanding what matters to the customer.

AI should support—not replace—the relationship.

The system can identify patterns and prepare questions. The account manager earns trust, listens and makes the judgment call.

The idea in one sentence

Better preparation creates better conversations. Better conversations create stronger customer relationships—and stronger relationships create opportunities to solve bigger problems.
